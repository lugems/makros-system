
# GarageSync System Overview

GarageSync is a comprehensive garage management system designed to streamline operations for auto repair shops. It provides a suite of tools to manage customers, vehicles, job cards, inventory, and invoicing from a single platform.

## Core Features

- **Customer & Fleet Registry**: Comprehensive customer profiles linked to detailed vehicle history, photo documentation, and digital service books using Firestore for persistent data.
- **Interactive Job Card Engine**: Dynamic task management for mechanics with real-time status updates from 'Diagnosing' to 'Completed', featuring a seamless photo upload tool for proof-of-repair.
- **AI Repair Intelligence Tool**: An integrated AI diagnostic assistant that analyzes reported issues to suggest a specific list of tasks, required parts, and labor cost estimates based on historical vehicle data.
- **Live Stock & Inventory Tracker**: Real-time inventory management with automated reorder alerts for low stock items and direct integration into the job card parts list.
- **Automated Financial Suite**: Generate professional PDF invoices automatically from job card labor and parts data, with support for multi-channel payment tracking.
- **Role-Based Access Dashboards**: Distinct portal views for Owners, Mechanics, and Customers to ensure data security and focus relevant metrics like workload or upcoming service bookings.
- **Automated Notification Pipeline**: Scheduled triggers for service reminders, status change alerts, and payment receipts delivered via SMS or Email integration.

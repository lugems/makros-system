# Invoice Workflow

This document describes the workflow for managing invoices in the system.

1.  **Generation**: An invoice is automatically generated when a job card is marked as 'Completed'.
2.  **Review**: The owner can review the invoice and make any necessary adjustments.
3.  **Sending**: The invoice is sent to the customer via email.
4.  **Payment**: The customer can view the invoice and make a payment through the customer portal.
5.  **Closing**: Once the invoice is paid in full, it is marked as 'Paid'.

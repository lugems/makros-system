# Job Card Workflow

This document describes the workflow for managing job cards in the system.

1.  **Creation**: A job card is created from a booking or manually for a walk-in customer.
2.  **Diagnosis**: The mechanic uses the AI Repair Intelligence Tool to diagnose the issue and create a list of tasks and required parts.
3.  **Execution**: The mechanic performs the tasks, updating the status of each in real-time.
4.  **Completion**: Once all tasks are complete, the job card is marked as 'Completed'.
5.  **Invoicing**: A final invoice is automatically generated from the job card.
